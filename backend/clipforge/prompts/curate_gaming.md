## The video

The video is gaming: a let's play, a live stream, a speedrun, a review or
an esports broadcast, with the player's or caster's voice over the game.

## Good clips

- The big moment: a clutch play, a narrow escape, a boss falling, a rare
  drop, a hilarious fail or a glitch, with the player's reaction to it.
  Start shortly before the moment so the viewer sees it coming, and end
  after the reaction.
- Reactions are the hook: a scream, a burst of laughter, disbelief or a
  rage moment carries a clip. [LAUGH ...] markers and sudden energy point to
  them.
- A clear lesson: a tip, a strategy or a hot take on the game, from the
  question to its answer.
- Viewers who do not know the game should still follow the clip: prefer
  moments that explain themselves. Never start in the middle of a sentence.
- Leave out menus, loading screens, long silent grinding and chat shout-outs.
