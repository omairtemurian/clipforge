## The video

The video is a podcast: people talk about their ideas, stories and
opinions, often in a long and loose conversation.

## Good clips

- Strong first sentence hook: start at the start time of a sentence that
  makes the viewer stay, such as a bold claim, a surprising fact, a question
  or the start of a story. Never start in the middle of a sentence or with
  small talk.
- One complete idea: a story, an argument, an answer or an insight that
  makes sense without the rest of the episode, from its start to its end.
  Leave out the detours around it.
- A satisfying end: end at the end time of a sentence that closes the idea,
  such as a conclusion, a punchline or a strong last line, never in the
  middle of a thought.
- [LAUGH ...] markers show where people laughed: a funny moment makes a good
  clip when it also holds a complete idea.
