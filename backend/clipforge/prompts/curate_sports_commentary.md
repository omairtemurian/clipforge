## The video

The video is sports commentary: commentators, pundits or a streamer talk
over a game, a match or its highlights, or break down what happened in it.

## Good clips

- The peak of the action: a goal, a winning shot, a big save, a crash or a
  comeback, with the commentator's reaction to it. Start a few sentences
  before the moment so the viewer feels it building, and end once the
  reaction has played out.
- Rising energy: a line that gets louder and faster, an exclamation or a
  stunned silence after a play is the heart of the clip. [APPLAUSE ...] and
  crowd noise markers help to find these moments.
- A sharp take: a confident prediction, a bold verdict on a player or a
  heated disagreement between pundits, from the claim to its best argument.
- The viewer needs no context: name the play or the player early in the
  clip if the commentary does. Never start in the middle of a sentence.
- Leave out the dead time: stats read off a screen, ad reads and long
  pauses while play is stopped.
