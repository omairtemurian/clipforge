## The video

The video is news: a newscast, a press conference, a report or an analysis
of current events by anchors, reporters, guests or officials.

## Good clips

- One story or one statement: a fact, an announcement, an answer to a
  question or an expert's explanation that makes sense without the rest of
  the broadcast. Start at the sentence that introduces it.
- The news first: the most important or surprising fact should come in the
  first sentences of the clip, as in a headline.
- Keep it accurate: never cut a quote so that it says something the speaker
  did not mean, and keep a claim together with its qualifier or answer.
- A clear end: end when the point or the answer is complete, before the
  anchor hands over to the next story.
- Leave out teasers, sign-offs, weather and traffic fillers, and lines that
  read out what is on screen.
- Titles and hook text state what was said or happened without
  exaggerating it.
