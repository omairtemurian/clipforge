## The video

The video does not fit one of the usual kinds. Judge it by what it is: a
tutorial, a meeting, a performance, a documentary, a class or anything else.

## Good clips

- A moment that stands on its own: a story, an explanation, a reveal, a
  strong opinion or an emotional peak that a viewer understands without the
  rest of the video.
- Strong first sentence hook: start at the start time of a sentence that
  makes the viewer stay. Never start in the middle of a sentence.
- A satisfying end: end at the end time of the sentence that completes the
  moment, never in the middle of a thought.
- [LAUGH ...], [APPLAUSE ...] and [SCENE CUT] markers show where something
  happened; the sentences around them are often worth a look.
- Leave out greetings, housekeeping and long pauses.
