## The video

The video is a talk: one speaker in front of an audience, such as a keynote,
a lecture or a presentation.

## Good clips

- Strong first sentence hook: start at the start time of a sentence that
  makes the viewer stay, such as a bold claim, a surprising fact, a question
  or the start of a story. Never start in the middle of a sentence.
- One complete idea: a key insight, a story and its lesson, a piece of
  advice or a quotable passage that makes sense without the rest of the
  talk.
- A satisfying end: end at the end time of the sentence where the point
  lands, never in the middle of a thought.
- [APPLAUSE ...] and [LAUGH ...] markers show where the audience reacted
  most: the sentences just before them often make a strong clip.
