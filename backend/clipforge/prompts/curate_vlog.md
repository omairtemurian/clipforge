## The video

The video is a vlog: a creator films their own day, a trip, a project or an
experience and talks to the camera about it.

## Good clips

- A small story with a turn: something goes wrong, surprises the creator,
  gets revealed or comes together. Start at the set-up of the moment and end
  once it pays off.
- Personality: an honest confession, a strong opinion, a funny reaction or a
  relatable struggle that makes the viewer feel they know the creator.
- A useful takeaway: a tip, a recommendation or a lesson learned, from the
  problem to the answer.
- The first sentence pulls the viewer in: a question, a promise ("you will
  not believe what happened") or the start of the action. Never start in the
  middle of a sentence.
- [SCENE CUT] markers show where the creator changed location or shot; a
  clip may span several of them when the story continues.
- Leave out intros, outros, sponsor reads and requests to subscribe.
