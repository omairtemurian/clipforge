## The video

The video is a sermon, a homily or another faith talk: a preacher or
teacher speaks to a congregation about scripture, belief and daily life.

## Good clips

- One message from start to end: a teaching, a story with its lesson or an
  encouragement that stands on its own. Start at the sentence that opens the
  thought, such as a question, a scripture reference or the start of a
  story, never in the middle of a sentence.
- Words that move people: a line of hope, comfort or challenge that a viewer
  would want to share or save. End at the end time of the sentence where
  the message lands.
- A scripture passage and its application belong together: keep the reading
  with the point the speaker draws from it.
- [APPLAUSE ...] markers and responses from the congregation (amen, applause)
  show the passages that reached people.
- Leave out announcements, offering appeals and greetings at the start or
  end of the service.
- Quote the speaker's words faithfully in the title and hook text; do not
  add claims that were not said.
