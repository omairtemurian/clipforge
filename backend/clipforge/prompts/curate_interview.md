## The video

The video is an interview: an interviewer asks questions and a guest
answers them. The speaker labels may not tell the two apart; follow what is
said.

## Good clips

- Strong first sentence hook: start at the start time of a pointed
  question, or of the strongest sentence of the answer when the question is
  long or not needed to follow it. Never start in the middle of a sentence.
- One complete idea: one question and its answer, or one complete part of a
  long answer, that makes sense without the rest of the interview.
- A satisfying end: end at the end time of the sentence where the answer
  makes its point, never in the middle of a thought.
